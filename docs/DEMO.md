# Lifora Hackathon Demo

## Starting scenario

- **ML Assignment:** due tomorrow, 40% complete, 3 hours remaining
- **DSA Quiz:** two days away, low preparation
- **Weekly budget:** Rs. 7,000
- **Remaining:** Rs. 3,500
- **Spent today:** Rs. 1,500
- **Available tonight:** 5 hours

## Demo flow

1. Display the current student state.
2. Ask Lifora what to do next.
3. Show Academic, Finance, and Balance specialist analysis.
4. Show the Coordinator decision.
5. Show the Best Next Action.
6. Show the Daily Plan.
7. Show the Decision Trace.
8. Change assignment progress from 40% to 90%.
9. Re-run Lifora.
10. Demonstrate replanning from the updated state.
11. Add an expense of Rs. 1,000.
12. Re-run Lifora.
13. Demonstrate the Finance Agent's reaction and updated coordination.

## Stretch

Run a What-If scenario without modifying real state. Make the hypothetical inputs and resulting recommendation visibly distinct from the persisted scenario.

## Demo note

The scenario and flow describe the intended MVP demo; they are not implemented functionality in the current dashboard shell.
